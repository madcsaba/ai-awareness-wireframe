Production Script & Storyboard (60 Seconds · 16:9)

“The Weight of a Click — Mindful AI & Our Environmental Footprint”

Scene 01 (0:00 – 0:08) · The Cloud is Made of Steel, Water & Power

Voiceover: “When you press Enter, your question doesn’t vanish into thin air. A single message on your screen spins up cooling fans and water towers inside physical data centers.”

Visual & Audio: Minimalist prompt window connected via fiber line to an architectural server hall. Soft acoustic keystroke followed by a gentle airflow hum.

Scene 02 (0:08 – 0:22) · A Mining Truck for a Croissant? (Model Sizing)

Voiceover: “How much energy it uses depends on your choices. Using a massive frontier reasoning model to rewrite a three-sentence email is like driving a forty-ton mining truck to the bakery for a single croissant. A compact or local model gets the same job done using thirty times less power.”

Visual & Audio: Split 16:9 comparison: 40-ton terracotta dump truck (~5.0 Wh) vs. sage-green electric bicycle (~0.15 Wh).

Scene 03 (0:22 – 0:36) · The Modality Escalator (Text vs. Image vs. Video)

Voiceover: “Output format matters even more. A short text reply barely equals a few seconds of an LED bulb. Generating a single image takes a quarter of a phone charge and a small glass of cooling water. And a five-second AI video uses enough electricity to ride an e-scooter for over four kilometers.”

Visual & Audio: Three rising editorial podiums: 0.2 Wh (LED) → 3.0 Wh (Phone + Water Glass) → 85 Wh (4.2 km E-Scooter).

Scene 04 (0:36 – 0:48) · The Backpack Trap & The Cost of “Thanks!”

Voiceover: “Then there is the hidden context trap. AI has no memory—on every turn, it re-reads the entire conversation from the beginning. Sending a separate ‘Thank you!’ at the end of a long thread forces the server to recalculate the whole mountain of text all over again.”

Visual & Audio: Courier climbing steps with stacked conversation blocks; adding a standalone “Thanks!” block spikes energy draw.

Scene 05 (0:48 – 1:00) · Mindful AI Campaign Call to Action

Voiceover: “Mindful AI isn’t about giving up technology—it’s about proportion. Right-size your model for routine work, write complete prompts upfront, and generate video only when it truly counts. The greenest computation is the one we don’t trigger unnecessarily.”

Visual & Audio: Interactive Habit Calculator and 3-point Campaign Pledge Poll. Warm harmonic marimba chord.
