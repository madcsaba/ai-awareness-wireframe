Eco-feedback animation

Reward for the least-energy, most environmentally friendly choice · wireframe

Trigger

  • small model or local/open model selected
  • mid-tier model — light confirmation
  • reasoning tier — contrast, not guilt

Animation in context

…the toast slides up from the bottom, leaf burst, live counters…
1Message (eco): “🌿 Greenest choice. GPT-5 mini uses ~100× less energy than Claude Opus — this reply ≈ 0,0001 kWh.” + live saving counter.
2Message (local): “🌿 Zero cloud energy. Your local model did this on your own machine.”
3Message (heavy): “⚡ This is the energy-heaviest tier (≈ 0,03 kWh). Fine when you need it — try the small model for quick asks.” — one-click switch, never blocking.
4Anatomy: 3 px progress strip → spring slide-in → ring showing share of worst-case energy (1% = nearly empty, reads as “good”) → kWh/CO₂ counter ticking up → leaf burst (≤ 12 particles, respects prefers-reduced-motion) → auto-dismiss at 6 s.

Behaviour notes

  • Positive-only by default: the celebration fires on the efficient choice; the heavy tier gets a calm amber notice, once, not every time (max 1×/session).
  • Numbers, not morality: always kWh + gCO₂ + a concrete comparison — never “you polluted”.
  • Streaks: the 6-slot bar fills per eco pick; a full bar triggers a stronger variant (“5 green picks in a row”).
  • Accessibility: aria-live="polite" announcement, reduced-motion falls back to a plain fade, dismissible with Esc.
